Our Digital Library Services

Explore modern facilities that make learning and reading easier.

Online Catalogue

Search our library collection online and find books by title, author or category.

Available: 24 hours a day.

How to use: Visit our online catalogue and search for your required book.

E-Books and Digital Resources

Read digital books, educational materials and learning resources from anywhere.

Available: 24/7 for registered members.

How to use: Log in using your library membership and select an available resource.

Study Room Booking

Students can reserve quiet study rooms for individual learning and group discussions.

Timings: Monday–Saturday, 9 AM–5 PM.

How to use: Contact the library desk and request an available time slot.

Printing and Scanning

Print assignments, scan documents and make digital copies of educational materials.

Timings: Monday–Saturday, 10 AM–6 PM.

How to use: Bring your document on a USB drive or email it to the library desk.

Free Wi-Fi

Enjoy internet access inside the library for research, online learning and educational activities.

Timings: During library opening hours.

How to use: Ask the library staff for the Wi-Fi access details.
