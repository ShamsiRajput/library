Digital Services

Readers' Haven Public Library offers modern digital facilities to support students, researchers, and community members both inside the library and from home.

24/7 Access

Online Catalogue (OPAC)

Search our entire physical and digital inventory from anywhere, check real-time shelf availability, and place holds on upcoming titles.

Timings: Available 24/7 online; in-library search kiosks open Mon–Sat, 9:00 AM – 8:00 PM.

How to Use:

  1. Open the Online Catalogue portal or visit a library search terminal.
  2. Search by book title, author name, subject, or ISBN.
  3. Sign in with your library card number to reserve available books for pickup.
Remote Lending

E-Books & Digital Audiobooks

Borrow thousands of e-books, academic journals, and audiobooks directly on your smartphone, tablet, or e-reader with automatic returns and zero late fees.

Timings: Instant 24/7 digital downloads for active members; 14-day loan period per title.

How to Access:

  1. Activate your digital library account at the front desk or online portal.
  2. Log in with your membership ID and PIN on your preferred reading device.
  3. Select a title and tap Borrow to read online or download for offline use.
Quiet & Group Rooms

Study-Room Booking

Reserve quiet individual booths or collaborative group study rooms equipped with whiteboards, power outlets, and presentation displays.

Timings: Mon–Sat, 9:30 AM – 7:30 PM (2-hour max slot, up to 6 people per room).

Booking Steps:

  1. Reserve a slot up to 3 days in advance at the Digital Help Desk or member portal.
  2. Check in with your library card at least 10 minutes before your slot.
  3. Collect the room key and return it at the desk once your session ends.
Self-Service Desk

Printing & Scanning

Print assignments, research papers, and documents in black-and-white or color, or scan pages directly to your USB drive or email address.

Timings: Mon–Sat, 9:00 AM – 7:30 PM (B&W: PKR 10/page, Color: PKR 30/page, Scanning: Free).

Steps to Print or Scan:

  1. Log in to a library computer or upload your PDF at the Self-Service Print Station.
  2. Select page layout, color preference, and number of copies.
  3. Confirm payment at the desk (for prints) and collect your output from Printer Bay A.
High-Speed Network

Free Public Wi-Fi

Stay connected across all reading halls, computer labs, and study zones with reliable high-speed wireless internet for academic and personal research.

Timings: Active during all library hours (Mon–Sat, 9:00 AM – 8:00 PM; Sun, 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM).

How to Connect:

  1. Select the network ReadersHaven-PublicWiFi on your device.
  2. Open your browser to load the library sign-in page.
  3. Enter your membership ID (or request a daily guest pass at reception).

Need Technical Assistance?

Our Digital Support Desk on the Ground Floor assists visitors daily from 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM. Send us a message if you have any questions.
